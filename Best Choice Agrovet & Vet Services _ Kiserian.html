Veterinary services

Non-surgical clinical care and preventive health programs for domestic animals, at our shop or on your farm. Tell us the species and the problem, and we will advise on the best next step.

💉

Vaccination programs

Cattle (FMD, anthrax, blackleg, LSD, brucellosis), sheep and goats (PPR, pox, CCPP, enterotoxaemia), poultry (Newcastle, Gumboro, fowl pox, Marek's, fowl typhoid), pigs, and rabies plus core shots for dogs and cats.

🐛

Deworming & parasite control

Roundworms, tapeworms, liver fluke and lungworm treatment with a schedule matched to your pasture and species, including dogs, cats and puppies.

🕷️

Tick, mite & lice control

Spray and dip advice, pour-ons and injectables for ticks and tick-borne diseases (East Coast fever, anaplasmosis, babesiosis, heartwater), plus mange and lice treatment.

🩺

Diagnosis & treatment of illness

Clinical examination and medical treatment for fever, diarrhoea, pneumonia, bloat, milk fever, ketosis, wounds, skin disease, eye and ear infections, and poisoning.

🔬

Lab & field testing

Faecal egg counts, blood smears for tick-borne disease, skin scrapings, California Mastitis Test, and rapid tests, with referral to partner labs for advanced tests.

🥛

Mastitis & dairy herd health

Udder health checks, mastitis treatment, dry cow therapy, teat dips, milking hygiene coaching and milk quality improvement.

🐮

Breeding & artificial insemination

Heat detection advice, AI with quality semen, pregnancy diagnosis, fertility problem management and calving-interval improvement.

🍼

Calf, kid & lamb care

Colostrum guidance, scours and navel care, early vaccination, weaning plans and growth monitoring.

🐔

Poultry health

Flock health checks, disease outbreak response (coccidiosis, CRD, Newcastle, fowl cholera), vaccination calendars, brooding setup and biosecurity for layers, broilers and kienyeji.

🐖

Pig, rabbit & small stock

Health plans for pigs, rabbits, goats and sheep, including mange, scours, respiratory disease, mineral deficiency and ration advice.

🐕

Pets: dogs & cats

Wellness checks, puppy and kitten vaccination, rabies jabs, flea and tick control, skin problems, nutrition and basic first aid.

🐴

Horse & donkey care

Wellness checks, vaccination advice, deworming, first-line treatment for colic, wounds, skin and hoof problems, plus feeding and body condition guidance.

🌾

Nutrition & ration formulation

Body condition scoring, dairy and beef rations, silage and hay advice, mineral supplementation and feed cost reduction.

🚜

On-farm visits & emergencies

Routine herd visits and urgent calls for sick animals, difficult-to-treat cases and calving support without surgery. Serious cases are referred to a surgical partner clinic.

🛡️

Herd health plans & biosecurity

Yearly calendars for vaccines, dewormers and dips, record keeping, quarantine rules and disease prevention for your whole enterprise.

🎓

Farmer training & advice

Practical sessions on dairy management, poultry keeping, drug withdrawal periods, safe drug use and animal welfare. Available for groups and cooperatives.

📋

Health certificates & movement advice

Guidance on veterinary documents, vaccination records and livestock movement requirements in Kenya.

Note: We do not offer surgical procedures. Cases that need an operation are referred to a trusted partner clinic.
